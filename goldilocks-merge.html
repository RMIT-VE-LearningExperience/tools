Goldilocks Merge

Drop in the full ledger, the benchmark file, and every per-task results file. It reconciles every claim, derives every count, runs the gate, and builds the provisional traffic light — deterministically, nothing judged, nothing sampled. Fix problems at their source file and re-merge; this tool never edits.
