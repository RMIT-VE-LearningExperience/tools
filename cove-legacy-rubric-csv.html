Legacy rubric CSV

Reads the assessment criteria out of a legacy assessor guide and writes a Canvas rubric import CSV. It only reads — your document is not modified, and nothing leaves your computer.

1. Add the assessor guide

Drop the .docx here or click to choose. It must be the original Word file, not a PDF or a converted copy.

Every criterion gets two ratings: Satisfactory (1 point) and Not Yet Satisfactory (0), with no rating descriptions and no scoring range. The marking guide is deliberately left out — a Canvas rubric is visible to students, and the minimum requirements are assessor information.

    
In Canvas: Course → Rubrics → Import Rubric, then attach the rubric to the assignment. Upload the downloaded file directly. It is UTF-8, which is what Canvas expects, and no byte order mark is added because a stray character in the header row stops Canvas finding the columns.
