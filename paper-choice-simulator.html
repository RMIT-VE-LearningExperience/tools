    Paper choice checklist
    • Start with your image intention and viewing context.
    • Decide on a paper base: fibre based, resin coated, matte, glossy.
    • Fibre based papers feel tactile, refined and non reflective.
    • Warm white suits softness and warmth. Bright white supports crisp contrast.
    • Natural white is a versatile middle ground.
    • Match the paper's character to the subject, or break that rule on purpose.
    • Smaller prints usually need less texture.
    • A wide border helps textured paper be seen as part of the work.
    • Test print before committing to a final edition.
    • Consider cost and environmental impact.

    This is a simulation for learning and planning. A screen cannot replace a physical test print. Always view a test print under the lighting your work will live under.
